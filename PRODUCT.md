# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The project owner, an engineering student building PROMETHEUS as a research-engineering portfolio project, and the people they show it to: reviewers, recruiters and other engineers who open the link to judge whether the work is real. Both are reading results, not operating a tool. They arrive with one question, "did the trained Creator actually learn to make better children?", and then dig into individual runs, children and replays to check the evidence.

## Product Purpose

A read-only lab notebook for the PROMETHEUS ecosystem. It shows, from recorded data only, how the Creator network was trained, whether it improved, how it compares with the baselines, and the full life story of every child: who designed it, why, what it was taught, what it learned, and how it behaved in a world whose laws changed under it. Success means a sceptical engineer can verify the headline claim in under a minute and trace any number back to a specific child or replay.

## Positioning

Every claim is verified and traceable. The headline uses unbiased, re-scored results; every chart links down to the children and episodes behind it.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, curious. The voice is a careful researcher's: it states results plainly, says "no" or "inconclusive" when that is the answer, and labels biased numbers as biased. Calm instrument-like confidence rather than hype.

## Anti-references

AI-hype landing pages with glowing gradients and "neural" imagery; SaaS KPI dashboards built from hero-metric cards; dark "hacker terminal" themes used for mood rather than legibility.

## Design Principles

- Evidence first: the verified result leads, and every aggregate links to the individual records behind it.
- Honest framing: show uncertainty (± std, seed counts) and say which numbers are selection-biased.
- Show the mechanism: replays, lesson plans and gene edits make the Creator's decisions visible, not just its scores.
- One instrument: the same visual vocabulary on every screen, so readers learn it once.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes; categorical colors are paired with labels or shapes so nothing depends on color alone; reduced-motion users get instant replay stepping and no animated transitions; everything is keyboard reachable.
